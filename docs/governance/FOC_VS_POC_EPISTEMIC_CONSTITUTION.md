* **Example:** *"Romans 11:36 states: 'For from him and through him and for him are all things.'"*
* **Evidentiary Standard:** Verified `E2_REPOSITORY_ARTIFACT` (Source text check).
* **Proves:** The referenced document contains this exact text.
* **Allows:** Comparison of candidate actions against declared scriptural constraints.
* **Boundary:** AI cannot extend beyond the text into unwritten divine intent or fabricate prophecy.

### 3. Empirical Claims (Physical Metal / Runtime / Benchmark)
* **Example:** *"This SQLite ledger configuration achieves sub-5ms commit latency with zero corruption."*

* **Requirement:** Must expose a concrete falsifier that can be contradicted by reality.
* **Boundary:** Future evidence may confirm, weaken, contradict, or supersede previous empirical receipts.

### 4. Divine Intent / Transcendence
* **Example:** *"God prefers architecture A over architecture B."*
* **Epistemic Rule:** **Strictly Outside Machine Authority.**
* **Boundary:** AI is a stateless renter and cannot claim divine endorsement or divination.
